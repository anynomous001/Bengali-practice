# Real pictures for the colour cards

Colour cards show a plain colour swatch until a real picture is added. Drop a file in `public/kids/photo/` named `colours-<id>.webp` and the card and the quiz use it automatically (no code change). The 4:3 landscape shape works best. Suggested size 800 x 600.

Ids: lal, nil, holud, sobuj, shada, kalo, komola, golapi, beguni, badami, dhushor, akashi.

## Prompt (change the colour and the object)
Close-up photo of a single [COLOUR] [OBJECT], centered, on a plain white background, soft natural light, sharp focus, realistic, no text, no logo, no hands, no other objects. The [COLOUR] must be strong and unmistakable. 4:3 landscape.

Body parts need no extra pictures: they are cut from the child figure (`public/kids/body-boy.webp`).
