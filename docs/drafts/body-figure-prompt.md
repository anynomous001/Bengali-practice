# Prompts for the body figure (Wizard Body Check)

## A. Image generator (PNG)
Full-body front view of a friendly 7-year-old child standing straight, facing the camera, arms relaxed and slightly away from the body, feet slightly apart. Clean semi-realistic children's-book illustration, soft shading, smooth vector-like edges, gentle warm colors. Plain sky-blue t-shirt, dark gray shorts, white socks and sneakers. Short brown hair, kind smiling face, clearly visible eyebrows, eyes, nose, mouth and both ears. Hands open with the fingers visible. Proportions of a real child (head about one sixth of the body height). Neutral, centered, whole body visible from head to feet with a little space around it. Transparent background, no ground shadow, no text, no labels, no props, no hat, no wand, no logo, no copyrighted characters, original design.
Make it 1200 x 2000 px, PNG with transparent background.
Make variations: boy, girl (same clothes style, hair in two small bunches), and one each with darker and lighter skin tones.

## B. SVG generator
Create an SVG of a friendly 7-year-old child, full body, front view, standing, semi-realistic children's-book style with soft gradients. viewBox 0 0 400 780, transparent background.
Structure it as separate groups with these ids so each can be clicked in a web page: head (head and hair), eyes (both eyes and eyebrows), nose, mouth, ears (both ears), hands (both hands with visible fingers), tummy (the belly area of the t-shirt), legs (both legs and feet). The groups for eyes, nose, mouth and ears must be drawn after the head so they sit on top of it. Use plain colors and linear gradients only, no filters, no embedded images, no text, no fonts. Clean paths, readable code. Sky-blue t-shirt, gray shorts, white sneakers, short brown hair. Original design only, no copyrighted characters.

## Checklist for the result
- Whole body visible, nothing cropped, centered.
- Transparent background (PNG) or no background rect (SVG).
- Face parts (eyes, nose, mouth, ears) clearly visible and not tiny.
- Free for commercial use (check the tool's terms) and keep a note of the tool used for the credit line.
