# Bengali Practice

Private practice web app for my Bengali students. Next.js (App Router) + Postgres, deployed on Vercel.

## What's in it

- **Email sign-in (no passwords).** Students get a one-time link. Only emails on the student list (or in `ADMIN_EMAILS`) can sign in, and the sign-in form gives the same reply either way.
- **12-month access.** Each student has an expiry date. When it passes they can still sign in but see an "access ended" page. Revoking or removing a student takes effect immediately.
- **Admin page** at `/admin` (admins only): add students, grant/extend 12 months, revoke, remove.
- **Three segments**, each with its own activities and progress:
  - **Reading**: alphabet (learn + letter→word and word→letter quizzes), recognition flashcards.
  - **Speaking**: letter sounds with a listening quiz, "say it aloud" flashcards (audio plays on flip).
  - **Writing**: trace letters on a canvas, build words from pieces, "write it down" flashcards.
- **Romanized English on/off.** A switch at the top of every lesson hides or shows the romanized spelling (like "ko", "kolom") everywhere: letters, example words, quizzes, flashcards, numbers and the writing activities. Each student's choice is remembered in a cookie. Flashcards adapt: with it off, the back shows the Bengali word and its English meaning instead.
- **Vowel signs (Reading).** A lesson on the 11 vowels and their signs (কার): the table, where each sign is written, a consonant-by-sign chart, words to read, 18 practice questions, and two flashcard decks. Text is generated from `content-drafts/build_matras.py` into `src/data/reading-lessons.ts`; it uses the same reader as the grammar topics.
- **Tense workout (Grammar).** Ten rounds (83 items) built from a class worksheet: tense cues, time-word cues, negatives, spot the mistake, fill the blank, check the sentence, two translation rounds, tense transformation and sentence combination. Choice rounds are auto-checked; writing rounds show a model answer and the student judges themselves. Text is generated by `content-drafts/build_workout.py` (forms come from the tense tables); the answer key for review is `content-drafts/tense-workout.md`.
- **Stories (Reading).** Seven short fables, each with vocabulary, then the story in layers (Bengali only, line by line with romanization and English, English only) and a quiz. Story text lives in `src/data/stories.ts` (review copy in `content-drafts/`), is only sent to students who have access, follows the Romanized English switch, and quiz scores are saved per student. To add a story, add an entry to that file.
- **Grammar (ব্যাকরণ).** A fourth segment with seven topics (persons and pronouns, verbs, tenses, karak, bibhakti, number and counting words, negation and questions): short lessons with tables and examples, then a practice quiz with saved scores. The text is in `src/data/grammar.ts`, which is **generated** by `python3 content-drafts/build_grammar.py` (verb tables are built from roots and endings by rule). Edit the script, rerun it, and also run `content-drafts/render_grammar_md.py` to refresh the review copy `content-drafts/grammar.md`.
- **Flashcards** use Leitner spaced repetition (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days). New decks are added in `src/data/decks.ts`; new activities are listed per segment in `src/data/segments.ts`.
- **Numbers & money** (reading quiz, listening quiz, flashcards) and **Build the sentence** use the lists in `src/data/numbers.ts` and `src/data/sentences.ts`. Sentences are placeholders; swap in your own.
- Conversations, directions, role-play and reading passages are not built yet.

## Setup

1. Create a Postgres database (Neon, Supabase or Vercel Marketplace) and copy its connection string.
2. Create a [Resend](https://resend.com) account, verify a sending domain and make an API key.
3. In Vercel → Project → Settings → Environment Variables, set everything in `.env.example`:
   `DATABASE_URL`, `AUTH_SECRET` (`openssl rand -base64 32`), `ADMIN_EMAILS`, `APP_URL`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`.
4. Redeploy. Tables are created automatically on first use.
5. Sign in with your admin email, open `/admin`, and add students.

Without `RESEND_API_KEY`, sign-in links are printed to the server log instead of emailed, which is handy locally.

## Online payment (Razorpay)

Students can buy 12 months of access at `/buy` ($40 by default, set `PRICE_USD`). The server creates a USD order, the student pays in Razorpay Checkout, and the server then confirms the payment with Razorpay before granting access (and emails a sign-in link). Buying again before expiry adds another 12 months. Each order grants access once, even if Checkout and the webhook both report it.

Setup:
1. In the Razorpay dashboard, request **International Payments** so USD cards work, and use **Test mode** keys first.
2. Set `RAZORPAY_KEY_ID`, `RAZORPAY_KEY_SECRET` and `PRICE_USD` in Vercel.
3. Add a webhook: Settings → Webhooks → URL `https://<your-site>/api/razorpay/webhook`, events **payment.captured** and **refund.processed**, and a secret of your choice. Put the same secret in `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. This is the backup that grants access if a student closes the tab right after paying.
4. Pay once yourself in test mode, then check the payment shows under "Recent online payments" in `/admin`, before switching to live keys.

Razorpay settles to your bank in rupees. Fees and conversion are shown in your dashboard.

## How access is protected

- Only enrolled emails (added by you or created by a verified payment) can sign in; sign-in links are single-use and expire in 15 minutes.
- **Every lesson page checks access itself** (`requireAccess()` in `src/lib/access.ts`), not just the layout, because layouts are skipped on client-side navigation. Access is read from the database on each request, so expiring, revoking or removing a student takes effect on their next click.
- **Lesson content is never in the public JavaScript.** Pages pass it to the interactive components as props after the access check. When you add new content (dialogues, vocabulary, audio), keep it in server code and send it the same way. Do not import content files from `"use client"` components, and serve audio from a route that calls `requireAccess()`, not from `/public`.
- Server actions (saving progress, flashcards) re-check access. `/admin` and its actions are admin-only.
- A full refund (`refund.processed`) takes the 12 months back. Partial refunds and disputes are not handled automatically; use `/admin` to revoke.
- Session cookie is `__Host-` prefixed, HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax. Security headers are set in `next.config.ts`.

Known limits: a paying student can still share their login or copy what is on their screen, and sign-in is not limited to one device.

## Run it on your computer (no setup)

You need Node 20+ and Git. No database, email service or accounts.

```
git clone https://github.com/anynomous001/Bengali-practice.git
cd Bengali-practice
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open http://localhost:3000, click **Student sign in**, and enter **admin@local.test**. The sign-in link is printed in the terminal (not emailed): open it and click Continue. You are the admin, so there is an **Admin** link for adding test students.

- Data is stored in a `.local-db` folder (delete it to start fresh). This only happens when `DATABASE_URL` is not set and you are not in production.
- To use a real database, an admin email of your choice, or Razorpay test keys locally, copy `.env.example` to `.env.local` and fill it in.

## Courses, lessons and progress

The app is organised as four courses: **Reading**, **Speaking**, **Vocabulary** and **Grammar** (Writing is paused and hidden from the menus; its pages still exist).
Each course is a list of modules, and each module is a list of lessons. All of it is defined in one file, `src/data/curriculum.ts`.

- **Course page** (`/learn/reading`, `/learn/speaking`, `/learn/grammar`): progress bar, Resume, what you will learn, and the syllabus with a tick beside each completed lesson.
- **Lesson page**: every lesson page is wrapped in `<LessonFrame lessonId="...">`, which adds the breadcrumb, course progress bar, the course outline (wide screens), and *Complete and continue* / *Next lesson* / *Mark incomplete*.
- **Progress** is stored per lesson in the `lesson_progress` table. A lesson is completed by the button, and also automatically when a story quiz, grammar quiz or tense-workout round is finished.
- **Dashboard** shows overall progress, a course card for each course, and a Resume button that returns to the course you last worked on.

To add a lesson: add it to a module in `curriculum.ts` (id like `reading/my-lesson`), build its page, and wrap the page content in `LessonFrame` with that id.

## Vocabulary by topic

**Vocabulary** is one of the four main sections: about 900 words and 330 phrases in 30 topics (family, body, food, fruits, vegetables and spices, sweets and snacks, house, clothes, animals, nature, seasons, days and months, colours, places, directions, transport, market, school, festivals, verbs, describing words, question words, greetings and more), grouped into eight modules, with a Review module of flashcards. Each topic is a lesson with:

- **Words** in clear sections, each with a listen button, romanization (follows the Romanized English switch) and the English meaning, plus *Cover the English* for self-testing,
- **Phrases**: about 11 sentences per topic that use the topic's words, with normal and slow playback,
- **Practice**: ten questions (Bengali to English, English to Bengali, listening). Scoring 7/10 completes the lesson, and the best score is saved,
- **Flashcards** for the topic (one deck per topic under Vocabulary → Review, spaced repetition like the other decks).

The **Word finder** (`/learn/vocabulary/search`) searches every word by Bengali, romanized or English.

To change the words, edit `content-drafts/vocab/*.py` (the example phrases are in `h_phrases.py`) and run `python3 content-drafts/build_vocab.py`. It checks every entry and rewrites `src/data/vocabulary.ts` and the review copy `content-drafts/vocabulary.md`. After adding words, run `npm run audio` to make their pronunciation clips.

## Pronunciation audio

Every Speak/Listen button plays a clip from `content-audio/` when there is one, and otherwise the browser's Bengali voice (the best West-Bengal `bn-IN` voice it has). A **Slow** button is next to the main one.

To get consistent, better audio on every device:

1. **Generated voice** (a few minutes, a few cents): get a Google Cloud Text-to-Speech key (or Azure Speech key), then run
   `TTS_API_KEY=... npm run audio`. Pick the voice with `TTS_VOICE` (`npm run audio -- --voices` lists Google's Bengali voices; Azure: `TTS_PROVIDER=azure TTS_REGION=centralindia`).
2. **Your teacher's recordings** (best for a Kolkata accent): `npm run audio -- --list` prints every text with its id; record each and save it as `content-audio/<id>.mp3`.
   Existing clips are never overwritten, so recordings and generated clips can be mixed.
3. Listen to the result, then commit `content-audio/` so it deploys.

Clips are private: they are served by `/api/audio` only to signed-in students with access.

## Light and dark theme

The sidebar (or the top bar on phones) has System / Light / Dark. The choice is saved in a cookie (`bp_theme`) so the right theme is rendered on the server with no flash.
All colours are tokens at the top of `src/app/globals.css`, defined once with `light-dark()`.

## Grammar, translation and exams

- **Grammar** is one scrolling lesson per topic (12 topics) with an "On this page" list, many examples, and a practice set at the end. The 10 tense workout rounds sit inside the Tenses module.
- Content is generated: `python3 content-drafts/build_grammar.py` (topic files in `content-drafts/gram/`) writes `src/data/grammar.ts`; `build_workout.py` writes the workout rounds. All Bengali was authored by the assistant and **needs the teacher's review**.
- **Quick translation** (`/learn/grammar/translate`): 10 sentences per round, English to Bengali or back, pick-the-answer or think-then-check. A score of 7+ completes the lesson.
- **Exams** (`src/data/exams.ts`): timed grammar (25 min), vocabulary (12 min) and listening (10 min) exams, pass mark 60%. Questions are generated per attempt on the server and stored in `exam_attempts`; answers are never sent to the browser and grading happens in `submitExam`. A reload resumes the running attempt; the timer follows the server deadline.

## Speaking: role play and impromptu topics

- **Role play** (`src/data/roleplay.ts`, `/learn/speaking/roleplay/<id>`): one scrolling lesson per scenario (shop bargaining, rickshaw/auto, restaurant, asking the way) with the situation, words, phrases you say, phrases you hear, two example conversations (one goes well, one goes wrong) and a "now you speak" practice where the app plays the other person. Completes at the end of the practice. Add a scenario by adding an object to `SCENARIOS`; the course and lesson pages pick it up. All Bengali is authored by the assistant and needs the teacher's review.
- **Impromptu topics** (`src/data/impromptu.ts`, `/learn/speaking/impromptu`): choose casual, professional, open or surprise, plus preparation time (30 s to 2 min) and speaking time (1 to 3 min). A server action hands out one random topic at a time; the page runs both countdowns with a sound between them and ends with a self-check list. The app cannot judge speech.

The server-side flow (signature checks, amount check, one-time grant, renewal, webhook-only grant, full and partial refunds) was exercised against a mock Razorpay API; a real test-mode payment has not been run.
